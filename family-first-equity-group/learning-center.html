Learning center

Prepared owners make clearer decisions.

Explore foundational real estate concepts before submitting property information.

01

What property acquisition means

Acquisition is the process of evaluating and potentially obtaining an interest in real estate. Review commonly includes ownership, condition, location, title, taxes, and intended use.

02

What disposition means

Disposition refers to transitioning or selling a property interest. A clear timeline, ownership record, and understanding of property condition can support an organized process.

03

Why property records matter

Accurate records help establish the property's history, current operations, known repairs, occupancy, and financial obligations.

04

Title, taxes, condition & ownership

These details can materially affect whether and how a real estate opportunity moves forward. Unknowns should be identified rather than guessed.

05

What owners should prepare

Start with the property address, type, ownership status, occupancy, general condition, known obligations, desired timeline, and your primary goal.
